The boundary

Dryer Math keeps duct-length arithmetic only. Every foot and elbow figure is a labeled norm, not a promise - lint, sagging flex and crushed duct change real airflow, and lint is a fire hazard. Follow the dryer's manual and local code over anything this page says, and clean the lint path on the schedule the manufacturer sets.

The claimed run assumes a straight shot to daylight. Every elbow spends some of it before the duct goes anywhere. Dryer Math does the honest arithmetic: what your route really counts as, how many elbows the claim has left in it, and how much straight duct the elbows leave behind.

Three panels. The run truth adds the elbows to the straight feet. The elbow truth counts the bends the claim can still afford. The straight truth prices the elbows first and counts the feet that remain.
